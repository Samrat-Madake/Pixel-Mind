Phase 2 — AI Pipelines is now fully implemented! 🧠

I have built the "Intelligence Engine" of PixelMind. All four primary pipelines are now ready to process your photo library entirely locally using your i5 CPU.

🏗️ Implemented Pipelines
Ingest Module (ingest.py):
Recursive folder scanning for all standard image formats.
Binary Dedup: SHA-256 hashing to skip already-indexed files.
Async Orchestrator: A transaction-safe queue worker that coordinates all AI tasks per image.
CLIP Pipeline (clip_pipeline.py):
Implements Open-CLIP ViT-B/32 for 512-D semantic encoding.
Optimized for CPU with torch.no_grad() and FP32 precision.
Face Pipeline (face_pipeline.py):
Detection: MTCNN for high-accuracy face bounding boxes.
Embedding: FaceNet (InceptionResnetV1) for 128-D unique identifiers.
Incremental Clustering: Implemented a Nearest Centroid approach that updates cluster centers in real-time without needing to re-scan your entire library.
EXIF & Geocoding Pipeline (exif_pipeline.py):
Extracts shot date, camera make/model, and GPS.
Offline Geocoding: Uses reverse_geocoder to resolve coordinates to place names locally.
Dedup Pipeline (dedup_pipeline.py):
pHash (Perceptual Hash): DCT 8×8 hashing to detect visually similar photos or slightly edited versions.
Hamming Distance: Automatic duplicate registration in the database for pairs exceeding the similarity threshold.
Thumbnail Utility (thumbnail.py):
Generates 256x256 cached JPEG previews for the frontend grid.
📥 AI Model Management
I have created a dedicated model_downloader.py utility. The models are currently being cached locally to ensure the app works 100% offline once set up.
